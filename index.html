Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Facebook Page Uploader ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our Windows desktop application to manage and publish content to Facebook Pages.

Important: Facebook Page Uploader is an independently developed desktop application. It is not owned, sponsored, or endorsed by Meta Platforms, Inc. Facebook is a trademark of Meta Platforms, Inc.

1. Information We Access

When you choose to connect a Facebook account, the Application requests permissions through Meta's official authorization process. Depending on the permissions approved by Meta and the Page tasks assigned to your account, the Application may access:

  • Basic account authorization information needed to complete the connection.
  • The names and IDs of Facebook Pages that your account is permitted to manage.
  • Page access tokens issued through Meta authorization, which allow the Application to perform the Page actions you request.
  • Content and publishing information needed to create or manage posts through the permissions granted by Meta.

The Application does not ask for your Facebook password. Authentication is performed through Meta's authorization interface.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Meta is used only to provide the Application's features, including connecting eligible Pages, preparing publishing tasks, publishing photos or videos when supported, displaying task results, and diagnosing errors.

Media files, captions, and schedules that you select are used to carry out the publishing actions you configure.

3. Local Storage and Security

The desktop Application is designed to store operational records, selected media paths, publishing schedules, and task history on your computer. Authentication tokens are intended to be stored using the operating system's credential storage facility rather than as plain text in the Application database.

No software can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting access to your Windows account and keeping the Application and operating system up to date.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

The Application communicates with Meta services through Meta's APIs to perform actions you request. Information may therefore be transmitted to Meta as necessary to authenticate your account and publish or manage Page content. We do not sell personal information. We do not share information with unrelated third parties for their own advertising purposes.

5. Retention and Deletion

Local task records and settings remain on your computer until you delete them or uninstall/remove the relevant application data. You can disconnect a Page and remove locally stored credentials using the Application's account-management features when those features are available.

You can also revoke the Application's access through your Facebook account settings. Revocation may prevent connected features from working. To request assistance with data deletion, use the contact method provided by the Application publisher.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is not designed for children and is intended for people who are permitted to use Facebook and manage Pages under Meta's applicable terms.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Application's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
